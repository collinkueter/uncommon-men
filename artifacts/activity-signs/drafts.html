Decisions to confirm before final print

Each sheet's QR and printed URL point to its activity on the live site. The updated activity catalog must be deployed before these signs are used. If a participant needs to enter a name first, the site returns them to the activity afterward. The rules below are proposed event rules based on published standards and common play. Please review the local choices before print.

  • Dumbbell Hold now has separate 10 lb and 20 lb activities and rankings. Each version assumes two matching dumbbells, one per hand; confirm there is a pair of each weight. The hold position and stop rule are proposed house rules.
  • Push Up: choose traditional Army push-ups or the current Army hand-release push-up. The two movements have different valid-rep rules.
  • Free throws: each attempt ends on the first miss; the longest made streak counts. Timed puzzles need identical sheets and someone to check each answer before stopping the clock.
  • Lightning / Knockout: standard group elimination does not fit the site's current head-to-head bracket; choose one format before print.
  • Bracket games: confirm match lengths and how draws are resolved. Pound the Nail needs identical starting nails and a marked flush finish.
